About the Lab

The Intelligent Systems and Collaborative Robotics Research Laboratory (ISCR Lab) at Purdue University Northwest develops intelligent robotic systems that collaborate with humans using AI, machine learning, and cognitive science. Our goal is to make robots effective teammates in homes, workplaces, and complex environments. My research focuses on advancing human-robot interaction, particularly through personalized robotic assistance and adaptive learning. A key challenge is enabling robots to understand and respond to human mental models in real time. I also investigate robotic assistance for space missions, where robots can support astronauts with routine maintenance and collaborative tasks.


The lab is directed by Dr. Sayanti Roy, Assistant Professor of Computer Science Department.

Research Areas

Human-Robot Interaction

Understanding how robots communicate, collaborate, and build trust with human teammates.

Multi-Robot Systems

Coordination, workload management, task allocation, and distributed decision making among robotic teams.

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, intelligent agents, and adaptive autonomy for collaborative robots.

Space Robotics

Human-robot teaming and autonomous systems for future lunar and space exploration missions.

Robot Communication

Natural language interaction, explainable autonomy, and maintaining human situation awareness.

Current Research Projects

Performative Autonomy

Investigating how robots communicate task priorities and maintain human situation awareness during demanding collaborative activities.

Multi-Agent Task Coordination

Evaluating interruption prediction and coordination strategies in intelligent multi-agent systems.

Lunar Surface Collaboration

Studying the impact of multi-robot team sizes and task criticality levels for future lunar missions.

Industrial AI & Vision

Applying computer vision and AI techniques to quality control and manufacturing processes.
